## Warning Signs You Need Gas Line Repair

### **Don’t Ignore the Red Flags**

Gas line issues should never be taken lightly. If you notice any of these warning signs in your home, it’s time to call Sunflower Plumbing immediately:

**Rotten Egg Odor:** Natural gas is odorless, but utilities add a sulfur-like scent for safety. If you smell this, evacuate and call us right away.

**Hissing or Whistling Sounds:** These sounds near your gas lines or appliances may indicate a leak.

**Dead Plants or Patches in Your Yard:** Dying vegetation around buried gas lines could signal an underground leak.

**Unexplained High Gas Bills:** A slow leak may be wasting gas without obvious signs.

**Appliances Not Functioning Properly:** If your pilot light won’t stay lit or you’re having issues with your stove or furnace, it may be a gas supply problem.

**Physical Symptoms:** Headaches, nausea, dizziness, or fatigue could result from gas exposure. If this happens, leave your home and call for emergency help immediately.

If you suspect a gas leak, your first step is safety: evacuate the premises, call your utility provider, then contact Sunflower Plumbing for professional gas line repair.

## Gas Safety Tips for Homeowners

### **Know How to Stay Safe Around Gas Lines**

Never ignore the smell of gas—leave immediately and call your gas company.

Don’t attempt DIY gas line repairs or installations.

Schedule annual appliance inspections for added peace of mind.

If digging in your yard, always call 811 before you start to avoid damaging buried lines.

Watch for signs like dying vegetation, hissing sounds, or pilot light issues.

Staying alert to early warning signs and calling professionals at the first sign of trouble is the best way to prevent gas-related hazards.
