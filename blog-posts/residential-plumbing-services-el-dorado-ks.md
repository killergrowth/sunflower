## What Homeowners in El Dorado, KS Actually Need From a Plumber

Plumbing problems don't wait for a convenient time. A leak under the sink, a water heater that stopped working, or a drain that's completely backed up — these are the kinds of issues that disrupt daily life fast. If you own a home in El Dorado or anywhere in Butler County, you need a local plumber who knows residential systems inside and out and can show up when it matters.

Sunflower Plumbing is based right here in the area. We handle the full range of residential plumbing services El Dorado, KS homeowners deal with — from routine maintenance to urgent repairs — without the runaround.

Here's a breakdown of what we cover.

---

## Leak Detection and Pipe Repair

Hidden leaks are one of the most damaging plumbing problems a homeowner can face. Water quietly seeping behind walls or under slabs can cause structural damage, mold growth, and skyrocketing utility bills before you even notice something is wrong.

Our team locates leaks accurately and repairs them correctly — whether that means fixing a pinhole in a supply line, replacing a corroded section of pipe, or repairing a joint that's failed under pressure. We work on copper, PVC, PEX, and older galvanized systems common in Butler County homes.

---

## Drain Cleaning and Clog Removal

Slow drains are annoying. Complete blockages are a crisis. Either way, the fix usually isn't a bottle of store-bought drain cleaner — that's a temporary patch at best and can damage pipes over time.

Sunflower uses professional-grade equipment to clear clogs in kitchen drains, bathroom drains, tub and shower lines, and main sewer connections. If you're dealing with recurring backups, we can camera-inspect the line to find out what's really going on — grease buildup, root intrusion, or a collapsed section that needs [sewer line repair](https://sunflowerplumbing.com/blog/sewer-line-repair-what-every-homeowner-should-know/).

---

## Water Heater Service and Replacement

Most homeowners don't think about their water heater until it stops working. At that point, they need someone fast.

We install, repair, and replace both traditional tank water heaters and tankless units. If your water heater is making noise, producing rusty water, running out of hot water faster than it used to, or simply aging out (most units last 8–12 years), it's worth having it evaluated before you're stuck with cold showers.

---

## Fixture Installation and Repair

Faucets, toilets, showerheads, garbage disposals, supply valves — these are the pieces of your plumbing system you interact with every single day. When they fail, leak, or just need an upgrade, we handle the swap cleanly and correctly.

We also handle new fixture installation for bathroom remodels and kitchen updates. A [plumbing professional](https://sunflowerplumbing.com/plumbing/) involved in your remodel from the start can save you from costly rework later.

---

## Water Line Service

Your main water line brings fresh water into your home. If it's leaking, corroded, or undersized for your household's demand, everything downstream suffers — pressure, water quality, and your bill.

Sunflower handles water line inspection, repair, and replacement. We also offer [excavation services](https://sunflowerplumbing.com/excavation/) when underground work is required, so you're not coordinating between two different contractors.

---

## Septic-Connected Plumbing

A significant portion of homes in Butler County — especially outside of El Dorado city limits — are on septic systems rather than municipal sewer. That changes how certain plumbing problems need to be diagnosed and handled.

If your home is on a septic system and you're experiencing slow drains, backups, or odors, the problem might be in the plumbing, the septic system itself, or both. We handle [septic services](https://sunflowerplumbing.com/septic/) alongside our plumbing work, which means we can trace the issue to its actual source instead of guessing.
