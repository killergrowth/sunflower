## What Goes Into New Construction Plumbing

Building a new home or commercial property in El Dorado, KS is an exciting process — but it comes with a lot of moving parts. Plumbing is one of the most critical systems in any new build, and getting it right from day one prevents headaches that can last the lifetime of the structure.

New construction plumbing is fundamentally different from repair or remodel work. There's no existing system to work around. Every pipe, fixture, drain line, and water supply connection gets planned from scratch and installed in precise coordination with other trades on the job site.

Here's what that process actually looks like when Sunflower Plumbing is on your project.

---

## Phase 1: Underground Rough-In

Before the slab is poured or the foundation is closed in, the underground plumbing goes in. This phase involves laying out the drain, waste, and vent (DWV) lines beneath the structure — setting the foundation for everything that follows.

In Butler County, soil conditions and frost depth requirements affect how this phase gets executed. Our team accounts for local conditions to ensure underground lines are properly bedded, pitched, and positioned before concrete covers them for good.

Mistakes made underground are expensive to fix later. That's why this phase deserves experienced hands — and why builders in El Dorado keep coming back to Sunflower.

---

## Phase 2: Above-Ground Rough-In

Once framing is up, the rough-in continues above grade. This is where supply lines, drain lines, and vent stacks get run through walls, floors, and ceilings before drywall closes everything in.

During rough-in, coordination with the general contractor matters. Plumbing needs to work around HVAC ducts, electrical runs, and structural elements — all at the same time. We show up when we're needed, communicate clearly with other subs, and don't slow the schedule.

This phase also includes pressure testing. Before anything gets covered, supply lines are pressure tested to confirm there are no leaks. Inspections happen at this stage in Butler County, and we're familiar with what local inspectors look for.

---

## Phase 3: Trim-Out and Fixture Installation

After drywall, paint, and tile are done, we come back for trim-out. This is when fixtures get installed — toilets, faucets, showers, tubs, water heaters, and any specialty equipment like tankless units or water softeners.

Trim-out is where the homeowner starts to see the finished product. We take care to protect finished surfaces, install fixtures cleanly, and verify every connection before sign-off.

For builders working on multiple units or spec homes in El Dorado and the surrounding Butler County area, we can coordinate trim-out scheduling across properties to keep your timeline on track.
