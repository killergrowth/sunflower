## Signs You Need a Kitchen or Bathroom Plumber

### **Catch Problems Early—Save Time and Money**

Many plumbing issues start small but can lead to major headaches. Watch for these warning signs:

**Dripping Faucets:** Small leaks waste water and inflate your bill.

**Slow or Clogged Drains:** Recurring backups point to buildup deep in your pipes.

**Leaks Under the Sink:** Puddles, water stains, or moldy smells signal hidden leaks.

**Running Toilets:** These quietly waste gallons of water and signal internal malfunctions.

**Low Water Pressure:** Weak flow may stem from pipe buildup, fixture wear, or undetected leaks.

**Foul Odors from Drains:** Often caused by sewer line issues or organic buildup that needs professional cleaning.

If you spot these issues, it’s time to call Sunflower Plumbing for quick, dependable help.

## Preventative Plumbing Tips for Kitchens & Bathrooms

### **Avoid Common Issues with a Little Maintenance**

Preventing plumbing issues in your kitchen and bathroom starts with a few simple habits. Avoid pouring grease or food scraps down the kitchen sink, as they can solidify and cause stubborn clogs. In the bathroom, using hair catchers in showers and tubs can help stop buildup before it blocks your drains. It’s also wise to check under sinks regularly for any signs of moisture or hidden leaks that could develop into bigger problems. Replacing aging faucet washers and toilet flappers is another effective way to prevent drips and running water. And to keep everything flowing smoothly, schedule periodic drain cleanings to eliminate buildup before it causes slow drains or backups. A little proactive care today can prevent costly plumbing emergencies tomorrow.
