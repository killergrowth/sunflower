## What Every El Dorado Homeowner Should Know About Their Plumbing

Most plumbing problems don't come out of nowhere. They build slowly — a slow drain here, a dripping faucet there — until one day you're calling a plumber at 7 AM because your basement is wet. If you live in El Dorado or anywhere in Butler County, knowing a few fundamentals can save you real money and real stress.

Here are 10 practical plumbing tips that every local homeowner should have in their back pocket.

---

## 1. Know Where Your Main Shutoff Valve Is

Before anything else — find your main water shutoff. It's usually near your water meter, in a basement, crawl space, or utility closet. If a pipe bursts, you need to kill the water in seconds, not minutes. Walk your house now and locate it.

---

## 2. Don't Ignore Slow Drains

A slow drain isn't just annoying — it's an early warning sign. It usually means buildup in the line, and if left alone, it turns into a full clog or worse, a backup. Don't pour chemical drain cleaners repeatedly. They corrode pipes over time. Get it looked at. You can read more about [why slow drains shouldn't be ignored](/blog/why-you-should-never-ignore-a-slow-drain/) and what they might be telling you.

---

## 3. Know the Age of Your Water Heater

Most water heaters last 8–12 years. If yours is approaching that range and you're in El Dorado, it's worth having it inspected before it fails — not after. A failed water heater on a cold Kansas morning is a bad surprise. Watch for rust-colored water, rumbling sounds, or water pooling near the base.

---

## 4. Test Your Toilet for Leaks

Put a few drops of food coloring in your toilet tank. Wait 15 minutes without flushing. If color shows up in the bowl, your flapper is leaking. A running toilet can waste hundreds of gallons of water per day — and it shows up on your bill.

---

## 5. Don't Put Grease Down the Drain

Ever. Grease solidifies in pipes and traps everything that flows past it. Coffee grounds, "flushable" wipes, and eggshells are also common culprits. If you're dealing with recurring kitchen drain clogs, your habits at the sink are likely the cause.

---

## 6. Insulate Pipes Before Winter

Butler County winters can get cold fast. Uninsulated pipes in exterior walls, garages, or crawl spaces are vulnerable to freezing — and frozen pipes burst. Foam pipe insulation is cheap. Burst pipes are not. Before temperatures drop, walk your home and wrap anything exposed.

---

## 7. Know Your Water Pressure

Normal residential water pressure is 40–80 PSI. Too high and it stresses every joint, fitting, and appliance in your home. You can buy a simple pressure gauge at any hardware store and check it yourself. If you're consistently over 80 PSI, a pressure-reducing valve may be needed.

---

## 8. Schedule Drain Cleaning Before Problems Start

Annual drain cleaning is one of the most overlooked maintenance items for homeowners. It's especially important in older El Dorado homes where buildup accumulates in cast iron or galvanized lines over time. Proactive service costs a fraction of emergency service. You can learn more about [how to prevent drain clogs](/blog/how-to-prevent-a-drain-clog-in-your-home/) before they become a real problem.

---

## 9. If You Have a Septic System, Pump It Regularly

A lot of Butler County properties — especially outside city limits — are on septic. The rule of thumb is pump every 3–5 years depending on household size. Skipping pumps leads to backups, drain field failure, and costs that can run into the thousands. [Routine septic pumping](/blog/benefits-of-scheduling-routine-el-dorado-septic-pumping/) is one of the simplest ways to protect your property.

---

## 10. Know When to Call a Pro

DIY has limits. Anything involving your main sewer line, water heater gas connections, or supply line repairs behind a wall should be handled by a licensed plumber. Botched repairs create bigger problems — and sometimes void your homeowner's insurance. Knowing [what to expect when you hire a plumber](/blog/plumber-near-me-what-to-expect-and-what-to-pay/) helps you make smarter decisions before the call.
