## Signs You Need Septic Service

### **Don’t Wait for a Backup—Know the Red Flags**

Septic system issues often start small, but delaying service can lead to major problems. Common warning signs include:

**Slow Draining Fixtures:** If sinks, tubs, or toilets are draining slowly, your tank may be full or your drain field saturated.

**Gurgling Pipes:** Odd sounds from your plumbing could point to blockages in the septic line.

**Sewage Odors:** A strong smell inside or around your property is often a sign of a leak or overflow.

**Standing Water or Soggy Spots:** Pools of water near your septic system indicate drain field failure.

**Unusually Green Grass:** If the area over your septic field is growing unusually well, it might be absorbing untreated wastewater.

**Frequent Pumping Needs:** If you’re scheduling pumping more often than usual, your system could be undersized or failing.

**Tank Replacement & Leach Field Installation:** We upgrade outdated systems and install efficient, code-compliant alternatives.

**Alternative Systems & Lagoons:** We also design and install lagoon-style systems and other advanced wastewater solutions.

If you notice any of these symptoms, schedule an inspection right away to avoid costly repairs and health hazards.
