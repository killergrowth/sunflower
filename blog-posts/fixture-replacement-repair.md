## Signs You Need Fixture Repair or Replacement

### **Don’t Ignore These Warning Signs**

If you’re unsure whether it’s time to replace or repair your fixtures, here are a few telltale signs that it’s time to call Sunflower Plumbing:

**Leaky Faucets or Showerheads:** Even a minor drip can waste hundreds of gallons of water per month, driving up your utility bill.

**Rust or Corrosion:** Fixtures with visible rust or mineral buildup not only look outdated but may also be damaging your water quality.

**Low Water Pressure:** Poor flow from your shower or faucet often signals clogging or internal damage in the fixture.

**Loose or Malfunctioning Handles:** Worn-out valves or stripped handles can make using your faucet or tub a daily struggle.

**Cracked or Damaged Sinks & Toilets:** Small cracks can leak and expand over time, leading to hidden water damage or mold.

**Outdated or Inefficient Fixtures:** Replacing old hardware with water-efficient models improves both aesthetics and performance.

These problems don’t just inconvenience you—they can lead to bigger issues if ignored. Let us take care of it before it turns costly.

## Tips to Maintain Plumbing Fixtures

### **Keep Fixtures Flowing Smoothly**

You can extend the life of your fixtures with a few simple habits:

Avoid harsh chemical cleaners that corrode finishes and seals

Clean aerators and showerheads regularly to prevent mineral buildup

Check under sinks and around bases for early signs of leaks

Tighten loose handles before damage occurs

Replace worn washers or cartridges at the first sign of a drip

Catching small problems early can save you from bigger, more expensive issues later on.
