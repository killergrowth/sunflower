## Is It Time to Replace Your Toilet — or Just Fix It?

Your toilet is one of the hardest-working fixtures in your home. Most homeowners don't give it a second thought until something goes wrong. When it does, the first question is almost always the same: *do I repair it or replace it?*

If you're dealing with a problem toilet in El Dorado, KS or anywhere in Butler County, this guide will help you make that call — and understand what it's going to cost either way.

## Signs You Should Repair Your Toilet

Not every toilet problem means it's time to shop for a new one. These issues are usually worth repairing:

- **Running constantly** — Most of the time this is a worn flapper, fill valve, or float. Parts are cheap and the fix is quick.
- **Weak flush** — Clogged rim jets or a partial clog in the trap are common culprits. A plumber can clear it fast.
- **Loose handle** — A minor mechanical fix. No reason to replace the whole unit.
- **Minor leak at the base** — If the wax ring has failed, a plumber can reseat the toilet and replace the ring for a fraction of the cost of a new toilet.

In general, if the toilet is less than 10–15 years old and structurally sound, a targeted repair often makes more sense than a full replacement.

## Signs You Should Replace Your Toilet

Some problems signal that a replacement is the smarter long-term move:

- **Cracks in the porcelain** — A crack in the bowl or tank is a serious issue. Water damage to your subfloor can follow fast. Once porcelain cracks, it doesn't get better.
- **Constantly clogging** — If you're reaching for the plunger once a week, your toilet may have a design flaw or a partially collapsed drain line underneath. Either way, it's time for a professional look.
- **High water bills from a phantom leak** — Older toilets, especially those installed before 1994, can use 3.5 to 7 gallons per flush. A modern 1.28 GPF toilet can save thousands of gallons per year.
- **Wobbling that won't stop** — If the toilet rocks even after a wax ring replacement, the flange or subfloor underneath may be damaged.
- **Age** — Toilets don't last forever. If yours is 20+ years old and has had multiple repairs, replacement is usually the better value.

## What Does Toilet Replacement Cost in El Dorado, KS?

Costs vary based on toilet model, existing plumbing condition, and labor. Here's a realistic range for Butler County homeowners:

| Service | Estimated Cost |
|---|---|
| Basic toilet repair (flapper, fill valve, etc.) | $75 – $175 |
| Wax ring replacement / reseat | $100 – $200 |
| Standard toilet replacement (labor + toilet) | $300 – $600 |
| Upgraded toilet (comfort height, dual-flush) | $500 – $900+ |
| Repair of subfloor damage at same time | Varies — get a quote |

These are estimates. Final price depends on what's found once your plumber gets in there — especially if there's hidden water damage or drain line issues involved.

## Repair vs. Replace: A Simple Rule of Thumb

If the repair cost is more than 50% of what a new toilet would cost installed, replacement usually wins. You get a warranty, better water efficiency, and peace of mind for the next 20+ years.
