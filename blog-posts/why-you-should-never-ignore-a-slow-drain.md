## What Causes a Slow Drain?

A **slow-draining sink, tub, or shower** might seem like a small issue—something you can live with for now. But ignoring it could lead to **bigger, more expensive plumbing problems** down the road. At **Sunflower Plumbing**, we help homeowners across **Butler County and surrounding areas**resolve slow drains **quickly and efficiently**, before things back up for real.

Here’s what your drain might be trying to tell you—and what to do about it.

Hair and Soap Scum – These are the usual suspects in bathroom drains, especially in showers and tubs.

Grease and Food Debris – Kitchen sinks often get clogged with grease, cooking oils, coffee grounds, and food scraps.

Flushed Non-Flushables – “Flushable” wipes, feminine hygiene products, and paper towels don’t break down like toilet paper and can clog your lines.

Tree Roots – In some cases, especially with older homes, roots grow into your main sewer line, slowing or blocking water flow entirely.

Hard Water Buildup – Mineral deposits from hard water can narrow your pipes over time, leading to reduced drainage.

## What a Slow Drain Could Be Warning You About

A slow drain is more than just a nuisance—it can be a **red flag for a bigger issue**, such as:

A Full or Failing Septic System – If you’re on a septic system and multiple drains are slow, your tank could be nearing capacity or malfunctioning.

Main Sewer Line Blockage – When multiple drains back up at once, it may indicate a serious blockage in your main line.

Hidden Pipe Damage – Clogs sometimes form around cracks, collapses, or misaligned pipes, which may require repair or replacement.

Impending Backup – A slow drain today can turn into a full-blown sewage backup tomorrow.

## Preventing Future Drain Problems

Use drain screens to catch hair and food scraps

Avoid pouring grease or oil down your sink

Only flush toilet paper—nothing else

Schedule routine drain cleanings, especially if your home has older pipes

Consider installing a water softener if you have hard water buildup
