## Common Signs You Need Water Heater Service

### **Don’t Wait Until It’s Too Late**

If you notice any of the following issues, call us for **professional water heater repair in El Dorado, KS**:

No Hot Water – Indicates a heating element or thermostat issue

Leaks Around the Tank – Could signal internal corrosion or pressure issues

Unusual Noises – Sediment buildup can cause rumbling or popping sounds

Rusty or Smelly Water – Tank corrosion or bacterial growth

Inconsistent Temperatures – Thermostat or element malfunctions

Rising Energy Bills – Inefficient systems consume more energy

**These issues don’t fix themselves**. Prompt attention can save you money and prevent water damage.
