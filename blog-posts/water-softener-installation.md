## Signs You Need a Water Softener in El Dorado, KS

### **How to Spot Hard Water in Your Home**

Not sure whether a water softener is right for you? Here are the most common signs of hard water:

**Chalky Residue on Faucets and Dishes:** Calcium and magnesium deposits create white stains and build-up on your fixtures.

**Dry, Itchy Skin and Hair:** Hard water strips moisture, leaving skin and hair dry and irritated.

**Soap That Won’t Lather Well:** If your soaps and shampoos aren’t lathering properly, hard water minerals are likely the culprit.

**Frequent Plumbing Repairs:** Scale buildup in pipes and appliances can lead to ongoing maintenance issues.

**Stiff or Faded Laundry:** Hard water damages fabric and causes clothes to feel rough and wear out faster.

If any of these issues sound familiar, it may be time to consider a water softener installation.
