## Common Toilet & Faucet Problems We Fix

### Resolving Everyday Issues with Lasting Repairs

Over time, toilets and faucets experience wear and tear, leading to inefficiencies and unnecessary water waste. Here are the most frequent issues we encounter:

**Toilet Problems:**

**Constantly Running Toilet:** Often caused by a worn-out flapper, malfunctioning fill valve, or faulty flush handle.

**Toilet Clogs or Weak Flushes:** May result from partial clogs, sediment buildup, or low water levels.

**Leaking Toilet Base:** Usually due to a deteriorated wax ring or loose toilet mounting bolts.

**Non-Responsive Flush:** A broken handle or disconnected flush mechanism can prevent proper flushing.

**Slow-Filling Tank:** Sediment in your water line or a failing fill valve can restrict refill speed.

**Faucet Problems:**

**Dripping Faucets:** Commonly caused by worn washers, seals, or cartridge valves.

**Low Water Pressure:** Often a sign of a clogged aerator or mineral buildup in the line.

**Leaking Faucet Bases:** Faulty gaskets or worn-out seals lead to water pooling around the faucet.

**Loose or Stuck Handles:** Aging handles can become difficult to use or break entirely.

**Unbalanced Temperature Control:** Malfunctioning mixing valves can prevent balanced hot and cold water delivery.

If you’re dealing with any of these issues, contact our El Dorado plumbing experts today. We’ll inspect, diagnose, and resolve your problem quickly and professionally.

## Tips to Prevent Toilet & Faucet Problems

### Simple Practices to Maintain Healthy Plumbing

Avoiding common plumbing issues starts with preventative care. To keep your toilets and faucets working their best, it’s important to be mindful of everyday habits. For instance, avoid flushing non-dissolvable items such as wipes, feminine products, and paper towels, as they can clog toilets and strain your plumbing system. In your sinks, regularly cleaning faucet aerators can help prevent mineral buildup that often leads to restricted water flow.

Periodically checking your fixtures for looseness—whether it’s the base of a toilet or the handles on a faucet—can help you catch small problems early. Replacing worn washers or gaskets before they fail can prevent dripping faucets or leaks from developing. And if you notice even a small drip or a toilet that isn’t flushing properly, don’t ignore it—these seemingly minor issues can often signal deeper plumbing concerns.

By staying proactive with simple maintenance and paying attention to small signs of trouble, you can extend the lifespan of your plumbing fixtures and avoid unexpected, costly repairs in the future.
