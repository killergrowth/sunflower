## When a Pipe Fails, Every Minute Counts

A single leaking pipe can dump hundreds of gallons of water into your home before you even notice the damage. In El Dorado, KS and across Butler County, Sunflower Plumbing responds to pipe failures every day — pinhole leaks behind walls, corroded galvanized lines, burst pipes after a hard freeze, cracked joints under slabs. The causes are different, but the urgency is always the same.

This post breaks down the most common types of pipe failures we see in El Dorado homes and businesses, what causes them, and exactly how we fix them.

---

## The Most Common Pipe Failures We See in El Dorado

### 1. Pinhole Leaks in Copper Pipe

Copper was the go-to pipe material for decades, and a lot of El Dorado homes still have it. The problem? Over time, aggressive water chemistry, high velocity flow, and age eat through the walls of copper pipe — leaving tiny pinhole leaks that drip slowly inside your walls or ceilings.

By the time you see a stain on the drywall, there's often mold or rot already forming behind it.

**How we fix it:** Depending on the extent of the damage, we'll either section out the affected length and replace it with new copper or transition to a modern PEX line. We'll also check surrounding pipe runs while we're in the wall — because pinholes rarely travel alone.

---

### 2. Galvanized Steel Pipe Corrosion

Homes built before the 1970s in El Dorado commonly have galvanized steel pipes. These lines had a good run, but galvanized steel corrodes from the inside out. Over decades, rust and mineral buildup restrict flow until you're getting a trickle from full-open faucets — and eventually the pipe fails completely.

**How we fix it:** Full or partial repiping with copper or PEX. If your galvanized supply lines are corroding, patching one section just kicks the problem down the road. We'll walk you through what a whole-home repipe looks like and give you a straight answer on whether a spot fix or full replacement makes more sense for your situation.

---

### 3. Frozen and Burst Pipes

Kansas winters hit hard, and Butler County is no exception. When water freezes inside a pipe, it expands — and pipes don't flex. The result is a split or burst that releases water the moment temps rise. Unheated crawl spaces, exterior walls, and garage-adjacent plumbing are the highest-risk spots.

**How we fix it:** We locate the burst section, cut it out, and replace it with properly sized fittings. We'll also look at whether the pipe run needs additional insulation or rerouting to prevent repeat failures next winter.

---

### 4. Slab Leaks

When a pipe under your concrete slab develops a leak, you may not notice until your water bill spikes, you hear running water with everything shut off, or you find warm spots on your floor. Slab leaks are one of the more serious pipe failures because water migrates through the slab and can undermine the foundation over time.

**How we fix it:** We use leak detection equipment to locate the failure without tearing up your entire floor. Depending on depth and pipe condition, we may repair through the slab or reroute the line above ground through walls or the attic — a technique that eliminates future slab access issues entirely.

---

### 5. Joint Failures and Bad Connections

Sometimes the pipe itself is fine — the failure is at a fitting, solder joint, or compression connection. Vibration, thermal expansion and contraction, improper installation, or simple age can cause joints to weep or blow out. These are common after DIY repairs or after a home has been worked on multiple times over the years.

**How we fix it:** We identify the leaking joint, cut it out, and install a proper fitting with the right technique for that pipe material. We don't slap tape on it and call it done.

## Signs You Might Have a Pipe Problem Right Now

- Unexplained spike in your water bill
- Low water pressure at one or more fixtures
- Discolored or rusty water
- Damp spots on walls, ceilings, or floors
- Musty smell in a room with no obvious source
- Sound of running water when everything is shut off

If any of these sound familiar, don't wait for the damage to get worse.
