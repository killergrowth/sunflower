## What Excavation Services Do We Offer?

Excavation isn't one-size-fits-all. Different jobs call for different equipment, different techniques, and different levels of precision. Here's what we handle:

### Trench Excavation
Trenching is the backbone of most underground plumbing and utility work. We dig clean, accurate trenches for water lines, sewer lines, drain fields, and gas lines. Proper trenching protects the integrity of surrounding soil and keeps your project on schedule.

### Sewer Line Excavation
When a sewer line collapses, roots invade, or bellying causes chronic backups, excavation is often the most reliable fix. We expose the damaged section, make the repair or full replacement, and backfill properly so the ground settles correctly over time. If you've been dealing with persistent [sewer line problems](/blog/sewer-line-repair-what-every-homeowner-should-know/), excavation may be the long-term solution that ends the cycle.

### Septic System Excavation
Installing a new septic system or replacing a failing one requires significant digging — and knowledge of Butler County's soil conditions and local regulations. We handle excavation for septic tanks, drain fields, and distribution lines. Learn more about our [septic services](/septic/) and how we approach these projects from start to finish.

### Site Preparation
Have a project that needs level ground before construction can begin? We provide site prep excavation for outbuildings, additions, driveways, and utility extensions on residential and light commercial properties throughout the El Dorado area.

### Foundation and Utility Excavation
Need a trench for electrical conduit, a foundation footing exposed for waterproofing, or a utility line rerouted? We work alongside general contractors and handle the dig so other trades can do their work safely and efficiently.

## What to Expect When You Schedule Excavation Work

Most excavation jobs in Butler County start with a site assessment. We look at the scope, identify any underground utilities that need to be located (always call 811 before any digging), assess soil conditions, and give you a clear picture of what the job involves.

From there, we schedule the work, mobilize equipment, and execute. Most residential excavation projects — trench digs, sewer line exposures, septic installs — are completed within one to three days depending on complexity and weather.

If you're facing a plumbing emergency that requires excavation, we move quickly. A collapsed sewer line or septic failure isn't something to sit on, and we treat those calls with the urgency they deserve.
