Older Butler County Sewer Lines Are Failing — Here's What to Watch For

Butler County's housing stock has a median build year around 1981 — meaning a large share of properties in El Dorado, Augusta, and surrounding communities still have original clay or cast iron sewer lines. These materials don't last forever. Clay lines crack and allow root intrusion. Cast iron corrodes and eventually collapses. When either one starts failing, you get recurring backups, slow drains throughout the house, and eventually a complete line failure.

Sunflower Plumbing camera-inspects every sewer line job before recommending a repair or replacement. You'll see exactly what's in the line before we propose any work — cracks, root intrusion, bellied sections, collapse. We don't recommend full replacement when a targeted repair will solve the problem. And we don't attempt repairs on lines that are too far gone to hold them.

Because we also handle excavation, we're not subcontracting the dig — we do the full job from camera inspection through excavation, line replacement, backfill, and compaction.

Common Questions

Sewer Line Questions Butler County Homeowners Ask

What are signs of a failing sewer line?

Recurring drain backups throughout the house, sewage odors inside or outside, slow drains in multiple fixtures at once, wet or sunken areas in the yard above the sewer line path, and gurgling sounds from drains after flushing are all warning signs. Any one of these warrants a camera inspection — catching a failing sewer line before it collapses is far cheaper than emergency excavation.

Do you camera inspect before recommending sewer line replacement?

Yes, always. Camera inspection before any sewer line work is non-negotiable for us. You'll see exactly what's in the line — cracks, root intrusion, bellying, collapsed sections — before we recommend anything. We don't sell replacements on guesswork.

How long does sewer line replacement take?

A standard residential sewer line replacement in El Dorado and Butler County typically takes 1–2 days depending on line length and depth. We excavate, replace with PVC, backfill, and compact. Yard restoration is included — we leave the property in reasonable condition, not just a trench you have to deal with.
