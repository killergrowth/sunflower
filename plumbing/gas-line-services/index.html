Common Questions

Gas Line Questions Butler County Homeowners Ask

What should I do if I smell gas in my home?

Leave the building immediately without operating any switches or electronics. Do not attempt to locate the leak yourself. Call your gas utility from outside or a neighbor's phone, then call us. Gas leaks require immediate evacuation — do not re-enter until the utility or a licensed plumber confirms it is safe.

Do you need a permit for gas line work in Kansas?

Yes. Most gas line installations and extensions in Kansas require a permit and inspection. Sunflower Plumbing handles permitting on all gas line work — we don't cut corners on code compliance because a gas leak is a life-safety issue, not just a regulatory one.

Can you run a gas line to a detached garage or outbuilding?

Yes. We regularly extend gas lines to outbuildings, detached garages, and additions on Butler County rural properties. The line is sized, permitted, pressure tested, and inspected before the gas is turned on. No shortcuts on gas work.
