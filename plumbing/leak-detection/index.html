Common Questions

Leak Detection Questions Butler County Homeowners Ask

What are signs of a hidden water leak in my home?

Common signs include unexplained increases in your water bill, damp spots on floors or walls, the sound of running water when everything is off, warm spots on a concrete floor (slab leak), mold or mildew odors, and low water pressure that appeared gradually. If you notice any of these, call us — catching a hidden leak early saves thousands in structural damage.

How do you find a leak without tearing out walls?

We use acoustic listening equipment and pressure testing to pinpoint leak location before opening any wall or floor. In most cases we can identify the exact location within a foot or two — meaning we only open what's necessary to make the repair, not a whole section of drywall or slab.

Are slab leaks common in Butler County?

Yes. Older homes in El Dorado and Augusta with copper supply lines under the slab are susceptible to pinhole leaks as the pipe ages and the soil shifts. Kansas clay soil movement during wet and dry cycles stresses under-slab lines over time. We see slab leak calls regularly throughout Butler County.
