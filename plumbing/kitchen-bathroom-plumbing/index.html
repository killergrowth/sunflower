Remodeling an Older Butler County Home? Expect Surprises Behind the Walls.

Kitchen and bathroom remodels in older El Dorado and Augusta homes regularly uncover galvanized supply lines that haven't been replaced since the 1960s, cast iron drain assemblies corroded beyond service life, and supply shut-offs that won't fully close. These aren't deal-breakers — but they're far cheaper to address while the walls are already open than to tackle separately later.

Sunflower Plumbing handles the full plumbing scope on kitchen and bathroom projects — rough-in, finish installation, appliance hookups, and any code corrections we find along the way. We coordinate directly with general contractors and tile setters when we're part of a larger remodel team.

For straightforward repairs and upgrades — a new kitchen faucet, a shower valve that's lost pressure, a garbage disposal swap — we're also the call for those smaller jobs throughout Butler County.

Kitchen Plumbing

Sink and faucet installation, garbage disposal hookup, dishwasher supply and drain, refrigerator ice maker line, and supply line upgrades. Bathroom Plumbing

Toilet installation, sink and faucet hookup, shower valve repair and replacement, tub drain assembly, and supply line replacement. For remodels: full rough-in including drain relocation, supply runs, and vent stack connections as required.

Bathroom Additions

New bathroom rough-in including supply line runs, drain tie-in, vent stack, and fixture installation. We assess your existing system's capacity before starting — adding a bathroom to an older home sometimes requires drain line upsizing or vent modifications that affect the scope.

Code Corrections During Remodels

Older Butler County homes frequently have plumbing that was done to code decades ago but doesn't meet current standards. We flag and address these during remodel work — not as an upsell, but because leaving known code violations in place creates liability for the homeowner down the road.

Common Questions

Kitchen & Bathroom Questions Butler County Homeowners Ask

What plumbing is typically involved in a bathroom remodel?

A typical bathroom remodel involves relocating or updating supply lines, drain assemblies, shower and tub valves, and toilet rough-in. In older Butler County homes we frequently find galvanized supply lines that should be replaced during a remodel — the access is already there and replacing them later costs more. We'll identify these during the initial walkthrough.

Can you add a bathroom to an existing home in El Dorado?

Yes. Adding a bathroom requires tying into existing drain and supply lines, which may involve cutting concrete or opening walls depending on the location. We assess the existing system, provide a scope and upfront quote, and handle the full plumbing rough-in and finish installation.
