Common Questions

Toilet & Faucet Questions Butler County Homeowners Ask

How much water does a running toilet waste?

A running toilet can waste 200 gallons per day — that's roughly $30–$60 added to your monthly water bill in most Butler County utility districts. The fix is usually a flapper or fill valve replacement that costs far less than letting it run another month.

Why does my faucet keep dripping after it was repaired?

Usually the wrong cartridge spec for your water chemistry. Butler County's hard water wears down cartridges faster than average, and some contractors use generic replacements that don't hold up to mineral-heavy water. We match the right parts to your specific faucet and water conditions.

Should I repair or replace a toilet?

If the toilet is over 20 years old and has recurring issues, replacement is usually the smarter call — modern low-flow toilets also cut water usage significantly. For newer toilets with a single mechanical failure (flapper, fill valve, flush handle), repair is straightforward and affordable.
