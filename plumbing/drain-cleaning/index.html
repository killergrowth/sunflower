Fix the Cause, Not Just the Clog

A slow drain in an El Dorado home is rarely just about what went down it last week. Butler County's housing stock skews old — a large share of properties in El Dorado, Augusta, and the surrounding rural communities were built in the 1960s and 70s, many still on original cast iron drain lines. Cast iron corrodes from the inside out, developing a rough, pitted surface that catches grease, soap, and debris at a rate that smooth PVC never would.

We camera inspect before jetting on any older home. High-pressure hydro jetting can rupture deteriorated cast iron pipe — turning a slow drain into a flooded crawl space. Knowing what's in the line before we put pressure to it isn't optional for us; it's standard practice.

If the drain clogs again three months after someone else "cleared" it, the pipe is the problem, not just the buildup. We'll show you on camera exactly what's going on and give you the honest options.

Drain Cleaning Services We Provide

Kitchen Drain Clearing

Grease buildup and food debris are the most common kitchen drain culprits. We clear the blockage and assess whether the drain line itself needs attention — in older homes, grease accumulates faster on corroded cast iron walls than most people realize.

Bathroom Drain Clearing

Hair, soap scum, and toothpaste buildup in tub and shower drains. We clear manually or with a snake depending on depth and severity. Hard water scale can compound the problem — Butler County's mineral-heavy water leaves deposits that narrow drain openings over time.

Sewer Line Cleaning

Main sewer line clearing for recurring backups, slow drain throughout the house, or sewage odors. Camera inspection before jetting on all older lines. If tree roots are the issue — common on properties with mature trees in Butler County — we'll show you exactly where and how much.

Hydro Jetting

High-pressure water jetting for stubborn grease buildups and root intrusion where the line can handle it. We never jet without camera inspection first — pipe condition determines whether jetting is the right tool or whether we need a different approach.

Most drain cleaning jobs in El Dorado and Butler County run $150–$350 depending on severity and access. Sewer line jetting with camera inspection starts around $300. Sunflower Plumbing provides upfront pricing before any work begins.

Why do drains clog so often in older Butler County homes?

Most of it comes down to aging cast iron drain lines. Homes built in the 1960s and 70s — common throughout El Dorado and Augusta — often still have original cast iron drains that have corroded and roughened on the inside, catching grease, soap scum, and debris far more readily than smooth modern PVC pipe.

Do you camera inspect before hydro jetting?

Yes, always on older homes. High-pressure jetting can rupture deteriorated cast iron pipe. We camera inspect first to assess pipe condition — if the line can't handle jetting pressure, we'll recommend a repair or lining instead of making a slow problem into an emergency.
