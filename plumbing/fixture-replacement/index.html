Fixture Installation & Repair

Upgrading or Replacing? We Handle the Whole Job.

Fixture replacements in older Butler County homes require more than just swapping hardware. Many El Dorado and Augusta properties have aging shut-off valves, corroded supply line connections, and drain assemblies that haven't been touched since the original install. When those fail during a fixture swap, you end up with a bigger job than you planned.

We inspect the supply valves and drain connections before installation begins and flag anything that needs attention. You'll know the full scope and cost upfront — not mid-job when options are limited.

We install customer-supplied fixtures and can also source and supply the fixture if you'd prefer. Either way, the work meets Kansas plumbing code and is done by a licensed, insured plumber.

Fixture Services We Provide

Sink Installation & Repair

Kitchen and bathroom sink installation including drop-in, undermount, and vessel styles. Drain assembly, P-trap, and supply line included. We inspect and replace shut-off valves as needed — not as an upsell, but because we're not leaving a fixture connected to a valve that might fail next month.

Shower & Tub Fixtures

Shower valve repair and replacement, showerhead installation, tub spout repair, and drain assembly replacement. Hard water scale buildup is a common issue with shower fixtures in Butler County — we clear it and install components rated for high-mineral water environments.

Garbage Disposal Installation

New disposal installation and replacement. We match disposal size to household usage — a half-horsepower unit is fine for a couple, undersized for a family of five. Proper wiring coordination if an electrical outlet isn't already present under the sink.

Supply Line & Shut-off Valve Replacement

Braided supply line replacement and compression/ball valve upgrades. A primary failure point in older Butler County homes — flexible supply lines degrade and burst without warning. We replace both the supply lines and any questionable shut-off valves as a package wherever we're doing fixture work.

Common Questions

Fixture Questions Butler County Homeowners Ask

Can you install a fixture I already purchased?

Yes. We install customer-supplied fixtures regularly. Just confirm the rough-in measurements match your existing plumbing before purchasing — we can advise on this over the phone if needed. If the fixture arrives damaged or incompatible, we'll let you know before starting the job.

Do old shut-off valves need to be replaced during fixture upgrades?

Often, yes. Shut-off valves in older Butler County homes — especially compression-style valves installed in the 1970s and 80s — frequently fail or won't fully close when turned after years without use. We inspect every shut-off valve during fixture work and replace any that are compromised before we leave.
