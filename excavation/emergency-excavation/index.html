What We Respond To

Emergency Excavation Situations We Handle

Collapsed Sewer Line

Sewer line collapse causing backup into the home is one of the most urgent plumbing emergencies. We camera inspect to confirm the collapse location, excavate, replace the failed section, and restore service — all in a single emergency response. Butler County's aging clay sewer lines make this one of our most common emergency calls.

Burst Water Service Line

Broken water service line between the city meter and the house causing uncontrolled water loss. We locate the break, excavate, repair or replace, and restore water service. Time matters — a running water main can undermine surrounding soil and create additional damage while you wait.

Septic System Failure

Surfacing sewage from a failed septic system or backed-up drain field is a health code issue and a property risk. Emergency excavation to pump, assess, and begin the repair or replacement process. We handle the full scope — tank, field, and plumbing connections.

Ground Collapse Over Buried Lines

Sinkholes and ground collapse over buried sewer or water lines — often caused by a compromised pipe that has been allowing water to erode surrounding soil. Emergency excavation to expose, assess, and repair the underlying cause before the collapse worsens or creates a safety hazard.
