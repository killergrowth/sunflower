Common Questions

Sewer & Water Line Questions Butler County Property Owners Ask

Why does sewer line excavation require a licensed plumber?

Sewer and water line work involves licensed plumbing connections at both ends of the trench — tie-in to the city main or septic system and connection back to the house. Using a plumber who also handles the excavation eliminates the coordination gap between contractors and means one license covers the entire job.

Do you call 811 before excavating?

Yes, always. We call 811 (Kansas One Call) before every dig to have utilities marked. In Butler County's older neighborhoods, as-built utility records are often inaccurate — we proceed carefully even after marking, especially near known utility corridors.
