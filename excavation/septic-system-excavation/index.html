Septic Tank Replacement

Common Questions

Septic Excavation Questions Butler County Property Owners Ask

How deep is a septic tank buried in Butler County?

Septic tanks in Butler County are typically buried 18 inches to 4 feet below grade depending on frost depth requirements and site conditions. Depth affects both the excavation scope and the riser height needed for future access. We install risers to grade on all new tank installations so future pumping doesn't require excavation.

Can a septic tank be replaced without replacing the lateral field?

Yes, if the field is in acceptable condition. We assess the lateral field before recommending tank-only replacement — if the field is saturated or at end of life, replacing the tank alone is a short-term fix. We'll give you an honest evaluation of the full system condition before scoping any work.
