Common Questions

Questions About Sunflower Plumbing

Who owns Sunflower Plumbing & Excavation?

Sunflower Plumbing & Excavation is owned by Brian Boswell. The company is locally owned and based in El Dorado, KS, serving Butler County and surrounding communities with plumbing, septic, and excavation services.

Is Sunflower Plumbing licensed and insured in Kansas?

Yes. Sunflower Plumbing & Excavation is fully licensed and insured to perform plumbing, septic, and excavation work in Kansas. All gas line work is performed by licensed technicians and meets Kansas state code.

Where is Sunflower Plumbing located?

Sunflower Plumbing & Excavation is located at 3910 W. Central Ave, El Dorado, KS 67042. We serve all of Butler County — including Augusta, Andover, Rose Hill, Benton, Towanda, Leon, and Potwin — and surrounding communities.

What services does Sunflower Plumbing offer?

Sunflower Plumbing & Excavation offers plumbing repair and installation, water heater service, drain cleaning, leak detection, gas lines, septic tank repair and installation, lateral field work, lagoon systems, and excavation throughout Butler County, KS.
